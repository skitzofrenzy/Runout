## What happens on your device

### Photos of a table

Runout can start from a photo of a pool or snooker table.

- You take the photo with your phone's own camera app, or you choose an existing picture with the system photo picker. Runout does not ask for camera or photo-library permission.
- Runout reads the picture's orientation so that it appears the right way up. It does not read or keep the location or any other details stored in the picture.
- The picture is analysed in the phone's memory to find the table and the balls. The temporary copy made for a new camera photo is deleted as soon as it has been read; anything left behind by an interrupted capture is removed the next time the photo screen opens. Runout does not save, copy or share your pictures.
- Runout does not try to recognise people. A photo may still show people in the background, so you may prefer to avoid including them.

### Settings Runout remembers

Runout stores a small set of choices in its private app storage on your device so that you do not have to repeat the setup: your skill level, your cue type, the game you play (including whether you use UK blackball rules), your usual table size, whether setup is finished, whether you prefer the Simple or Pro display, and that you accepted the Terms of Use and which version.

These choices are not linked to your name or to any account, and the developer cannot see them.

### Other things Runout uses, without storing them

- **Tilt sensor and microphone.** In this version Runout does not read your phone's tilt sensor or its microphone. The table level check and the break-speed meter use built-in sample data.
- **Everything else you enter.** Table layouts, drill scores, the scorekeeper, the shot clock and similar tools run on your device. Today they are not saved by Runout after you close it, and nothing is sent anywhere.

### Permissions

Runout does not ask you for any permission on Android or iOS. In particular it does not use your microphone, your location, your contacts, or your files.

## Sharing and sale

Runout does not share, sell, rent or disclose any information, because it has none. It contains no advertising or analytics code from other companies.

If you open a web address from Runout in your browser (for example a link to an official rulebook), that website's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

## How long things are kept, and how to delete them

Settings stay on your device until you remove them. You can remove them by clearing Runout's storage in your system settings or by uninstalling Runout. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

Android: automatic backup is switched off for Runout, so its settings are not copied to your Google account backup. On iOS, your device backup settings decide whether app data is included in a backup you make.

## Security

Everything stays inside Runout's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. Please keep your device locked and up to date. Because nothing leaves the device, there is no server that could be breached.

## Children
