## 1. What Runout is

Runout is a free training aid for pool and snooker. You photograph a table or build a layout by hand, and Runout shows estimated shot lines, cue-ball paths, spin and speed suggestions and multi-shot plans. It also contains rules summaries, drills, a scorekeeper and a shot clock.

Runout is free of charge and published as open source. It has no accounts, no adverts and no purchases, it brings in no revenue, and it does not need an internet connection.

## 4. Runout gives estimates, not guarantees

- Shot lines, cue-ball paths, speed and spin suggestions, heat zones, run-out plans and break-speed readings are **calculated estimates** from a simplified model of the table, the balls and the cloth.
- Real tables, cloth, cushions, balls, lighting, your cue and your stroke all differ from the model. A photo can be misread: the table corners and the ball positions it finds may be wrong, which is why Runout asks you to check and correct them.
- Runout does not guarantee that any shot will go in, that any plan will work, or that any measurement is accurate.
- The rules summaries are short plain-language summaries. They are not the official rules and may be out of date. The official rulebooks (for example from the WPA and the WPBSA) decide any question of rules.
- Runout is for practice. Most leagues and tournaments ban electronic aids during a match, so do not use it where that is not allowed.
