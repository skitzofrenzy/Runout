# Handover

**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

Where the project stands, what has been decided and why, and what to do next. Update this file whenever the state of the project changes.

## Status

All six roadmap milestones — 0 (Research & prototype), 1 (MVP), 2 (Photo detection), 3 (Strategy), 4 (Calibration), and 5 (Extras) — are implemented and verified in `:engine` and `:composeApp` (with `iosApp/` Swift entry sources, `Info.plist`, and configuration; `.xcodeproj` generation pending macOS):

- **Milestone 0 & 1 (`:engine` + `:composeApp`):** SI table/ball geometry (`TableSpec`, `Ball`, `TableLayout`), analytic motion laws ($5/7$ stun speed, $t = 2u_0 / (7\mu_s g)$, $d = v^2 / (2\mu_r g)$), ghost-ball aiming (`GhostBallAiming`), 90°/30°/draw cue-ball paths (`CueBallPath`), `PlayerProfile` (`1–10` speed-scale mapping and questionnaire calibration), `BallSetProfile` (preset and colour-to-number swaps), 2-ball position-play ranking (`OneShotPlanner`), an interactive drag-and-drop table editor (`PoolTableCanvas`), and an offline searchable `Rules & FAQ` browser (`RulesRepository`, `RulesSearchEngine`) across 8-Ball, 9-Ball, 10-Ball, and Snooker.
- **Milestone 2 (`:engine` + `:composeApp`):** 8-DOF DLT planar homography (`PerspectiveHomography`) with $z = R$ 3D ball-height ray-plane correction, Hungarian minimum-cost bipartite ball identification (`BallIdentificationAndAssignment`), $2R$ overlap push-apart, frozen-ball detection, multi-signal `GameTypeSuggester`, an in-memory `expect`/`actual` camera capture contract (`PhotoCaptureContract` + Android/iOS `PlatformCameraController` stripping EXIF/GPS metadata), and `PhotoCaptureAndConfirmScreen` with framing guide, draggable 4-corner handles, ball renumbering, and layout handoff.
- **Milestone 3 (`:engine` + `:composeApp`):**
  - `SpinPhysics`: miscue-safe 2D `TipOffset` ($\le 0.50 R$), shaft-dependent squirt, elevation-dependent swerve, speed- and friction-dependent cut/spin throw, `SpinCompensatedAim`, and Han (2005)-inspired spin cushion rebound with speed-dependent restitution (`traceCueBallPathWithSpin`).
  - `ExecutionErrorModel`: speed- and clearance-aware pocket acceptance window plus deterministic 9-point sigma-point uncertainty propagation (`ShotUncertaintyResult` with `makeProbability`, `scratchProbability`, `landingCloud`, `meanLandingPosition`, and 95% `landingSpreadRadiusMeters`).
  - `HeatZoneMap` / `HeatZoneGenerator`: cached spatial position-value grids over playable cloth for upcoming legal target balls.
  - `ProblemBallAnalyzer`: identifies `NO_CLEAR_POCKET`, `CLUSTERED_NEIGHBOURS`, and `FROZEN_TO_RAIL` balls, selects an open insurance ball, and detects cue-ball trajectory breakouts (`BreakoutOpportunity`).
  - `MultiShotPlanner`: expectimax beam-search planner (`1–5` shots ahead plus `deepAnalysis` full-rack mode), plain-English tactical `explanationReasons`, and `evaluateAgainstGreedyBaseline` self-play verification proving multi-shot planning achieves a higher run-out rate than a greedy 1-shot baseline.
  - Strategy UI (`TableCanvas.kt`, `App.kt`): cloth position heat-zone grid overlay, landing cloud & 95% spread ring, multi-step shot sequence overlay, problem-ball warning halos, interactive 2D `CueTipSelectorCanvas` with squirt/swerve/throw compensation readouts, lookahead horizon chips (`1-Shot`..`Deep Run-Out`), problem-ball & breakout cards, and multi-shot run-out plan cards.
- **Milestone 4 (`:engine` + `:composeApp`):**
  - `VideoBallTracker`: 5-tap `[1, 3, 4, 3, 1] / 12` + 6-frame orthogonal-basis 100/120 Hz luma flicker normalization (`normalizeFrameBrightness`), independent-axis 2D `[pos, vel]` Kalman filter with maneuver gating on cushion impacts and intra-segment RTS smoothing (`trackBallTrajectory`), and kinematic event detection (`SLIDE_TO_ROLL_TRANSITION`, `CUSHION_BOUNCE`, `BALL_STOP`).
  - `TableCalibrationFitter`: MAD-outlier-trimmed, physically bounded parameter fitting across all 5 drills (lag $\mu_r$, stun $\mu_s$, head-on cushion $e_c$, bank-spin coupling $k_{\text{spin}}$, and golden-section half-ball throw $\mu_b$) plus `evaluateHeldOutPredictionAccuracy` demonstrating substantial held-out RMS error reduction over uncalibrated defaults.
  - `TableLevelAndProfiles`: paired $0^\circ / 180^\circ$ rotate-in-place bias cancellation (`cancelSensorBias180`), $3 \times 3$ `TableSlopeMap`, slow-roll lateral drift slope inversion (`estimateSlopeFromSlowRoll`), sloped stopping predictor (`predictSlowRollWithSlope`), and `SavedTableProfile`.
  - Platform & Calibration UI (`TiltSensorContract.kt`, Android/iOS `PlatformTiltSensorController`, `CalibrationScreen.kt`, `App.kt`): 3 benchmark calibration sessions (`Fast Tournament 9-ft`, `Slow Bar-Box 7-ft`, `Unlevel Home 8-ft`), overhead tracking & flicker summary, fitted-vs-default comparison table, held-out validation card, $3 \times 3$ slope grid with slow-roll check, and one-tap `"Apply to Table Planner"` profile switching.
- **Milestone 5 (`:engine` + `:composeApp`):**
  - `SnookerStrategyPlanner` (`com.runout.engine.snooker`): WPBSA spot geometry (`SnookerSpots` with `BAULK_LINE_X_METERS = 0.737 m`, `D_RADIUS_METERS = 0.292 m`), `SnookerScoreState` (`pointsRemainingOnTable`, `snookersRequiredCount`, `isFrameBallAvailable`), beam-search `planBreakSequence` alternating reds and WPBSA-re-spotted colours (`2..7` ascending endgame clearance), `findBestSafety` baulk-return and cushion-escape evaluator, and `SnookerTacticalMode` (`ATTACK_BREAK_BUILD`, `CONTAINMENT_SAFETY`, `PLAY_FOR_SNOOKERS`).

  - Practice tools & 6-tab UI (`PracticeToolsContract.kt`, Android/iOS `PlatformBreakAudioController`, `ExtrasAndToolsScreen.kt`, 6-tab `App.kt`): in-memory transient PCM break audio bridge, 8 interactive tutorials (`"Try on Table Planner"`), 5 scored drills with interactive practice log, Pool Race & Snooker scorekeepers, Shot Clock with once-per-rack `+30s` extensions, and the `"Tools & Coach"` tab.

## Decisions so far

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Name: Runout | A pool term for clearing the table, which is what the planner helps with. Short and easy to search for. |
| MIT licence | Simple and permissive. |
| Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) with Compose Multiplatform for Android and iOS ([ADR-0001](adr/0001-cross-platform-kmp-compose.md)) | Shares the `engine`, 2D table canvas, UI, and Room database across Android and iOS while keeping direct native access to CameraX/LiteRT on Android and AVFoundation/CoreML on iOS. |
| Physics and planner in a pure KMP `engine` module (`commonMain`) | Unit-testable on the JVM without a device, and compiles natively for both Android and iOS. |
| Still photos, not a live AR overlay | Plain cloth gives AR tracking little to lock onto, so overlays drift. |
| Apache-2.0 detector (YOLOX or EfficientDet-Lite), not Ultralytics YOLO | Ultralytics is AGPL-3.0, which would force the app to be distributed under the AGPL. |
| Table calibration from overhead video | Handheld, angled video is too inaccurate for fitting cloth and cushion parameters. |
| Spin inferred from ball paths | Phone cameras can't reliably see a ball rotate. |
| 8, 9 and 10-ball first; snooker strategy last | Safety play dominates snooker, which makes its strategy much harder. |
| Original rule summaries with bidirectional synonym search | Official rulebooks are copyrighted; original summaries with `WPA`/`WPBSA` citations stay clean legally while synonym expansion (`BIH`, `kitchen`, `scratch`) makes queries effortless offline. |
| Fully offline | No accounts or servers to run, and it works in any pool hall. |
| **M1:** `PlayerProfile` questionnaire + `1–10` linear speed scale & `BallSetProfile` swaps | Converts subjective speed terms into consistent SI velocities per skill/shaft class and handles custom or TV ball palettes without altering physics rules. |
| **M1:** Normalized `0..1` shot score with 2-ball shape bonus (`+0.25`) & low-draw penalty (`-0.15`) | Keeps scoring bounded and comparable across layouts while preventing `OneShotPlanner` from recommending heavy draw shots to beginners with low `maxEffectiveDrawRatio`. |
| **M2:** 4-corner 8-DOF DLT planar homography with interactive corner handles | Rectifies angled single-table photos without needing a printed calibration checkerboard on the cloth, with manual handle drag as a resilient fallback. |
| **M2:** 3D ball-height ray-plane correction at $z = R$ (`imageToBallCenterMeters`) | An elevated ball's visual centre at $z = R$ projects outward onto the $z = 0$ cloth plane; intersecting the camera ray with $z = R$ eliminates multi-centimetre parallax error near the table edges. |
| **M2:** Hungarian bipartite ball identification + $2R$ overlap push-apart + $2\text{ mm}$ frozen detection | Guarantees globally optimal 1-to-1 ball numbering even with ambiguous colour pairs (`RED`/`MAROON`, `YELLOW`/`ORANGE`), prevents impossible overlapping balls, and flags cushion/cluster contacts for user review. |
| **M2:** EXIF/GPS metadata stripping + user-confirmed `GameTypeSuggester` | Protects player privacy before any image handling and prevents misclassifying partial racks without user confirmation. |
| **M3:** Closed-form squirt/swerve/throw + Han (2005)-inspired english cushion model ([ADR-0002](adr/0002-event-spin-physics-and-beam-planner.md)) | Captures side-spin deflection, elevated-cue swerve, gearing/cut throw, and running/reverse rail rebound angles while staying far below the `< 0.1 ms` per-evaluation runtime budget. |
| **M3:** Deterministic 9-point sigma-point error propagation (`ExecutionErrorModel`) | Replaces non-deterministic random Monte Carlo sampling with a symmetric 9-point stencil over $(\Delta\phi, \Delta v, \Delta a, \Delta b)$ paired with analytical pocket-acceptance survival, making unit tests 100% reproducible and fast. |
| **M3:** Cached spatial `HeatZoneMap` grids (`HeatZoneGenerator`) | Decouples position quality at $(x, y)$ from arrival path so beam search reuses $O(1)$ grid lookups and the UI renders green/amber/red cloth position zones directly. |
| **M3:** Expectimax beam search (`MultiShotPlanner`) with `ProblemBallAnalyzer` breakout/insurance scoring | Keeps 2–5 shot lookahead (plus deep full-rack mode) interactive on mobile while rewarding early breakouts when an open insurance ball exists and generating human-readable tactical explanations. |
| **M4:** 100/120 Hz luma flicker normalization + 2D `[pos, vel]` Kalman tracking with maneuver gating & RTS smoothing (`VideoBallTracker`, [ADR-0003](adr/0003-video-calibration-and-table-level-fitting.md)) | Removes mains-frequency brightness oscillations without flattening slow exposure drift, prevents differentiation from amplifying $\pm 1.8\text{ mm}$ per-frame detection jitter by $60\text{–}240\times$, and splits cushion bounces cleanly so backward smoothing does not smear velocity across rail impacts. |
| **M4:** Bounded MAD-outlier-trimmed least-squares drill parameter fitting (`TableCalibrationFitter`, [ADR-0003](adr/0003-video-calibration-and-table-level-fitting.md)) | Rejects mis-tracked or bumped drill trials via median absolute deviation fences and clamps $(\mu_r, \mu_s, e_c, k_{\text{spin}}, \mu_b)$ to physical intervals so noisy user sessions never diverge or emit negative friction/restitution $> 1$. |
| **M4:** Paired $0^\circ / 180^\circ$ rotate-in-place tilt bias cancellation + slow-roll lateral drift slope inversion (`TableLevelAndProfiles`, [ADR-0003](adr/0003-video-calibration-and-table-level-fitting.md)) | Taking $(\theta_0 - \theta_{180}) / 2$ across a $3 \times 3$ grid cancels phone accelerometer zero-bias and camera-bump tilt completely, while inverting slow-roll sideways drift $\Delta y = \frac{5}{7} \frac{L_{\text{roll}} \sin\theta}{\mu_r}$ resolves sub-sensor roll-off below the $\sim 0.1^\circ$ MEMS floor. |
| **M5:** WPBSA red–colour alternation with colour re-spotting & baulk-return safety evaluation (`SnookerStrategyPlanner`, [ADR-0004](adr/0004-snooker-break-safety-planner-and-acoustic-break-speed.md)) | Unlike pool where potted balls remain off-table, snooker alternates reds (`1 pt`, removed) and nominated colours (`2–7 pts`, re-spotted on WPBSA spots while reds remain) before ascending `2..7` endgame clearance; scoring baulk containment (`x <= 0.737 m`) and deficit snookers (`PLAY_FOR_SNOOKERS`) prevents low-percentage attacks when safety or snookers govern frame outcome. |
| **M5:** Acoustic break-speed estimation with speed-of-sound & sliding-friction corrections (`BreakSpeedEstimator`, `PlatformBreakAudioController`, [ADR-0004](adr/0004-snooker-break-safety-planner-and-acoustic-break-speed.md)) | Removing microphone sound-travel differential $(d_{\text{mic,rack}} - d_{\text{mic,cue}}) / 343.0$ and restoring cloth sliding-friction loss $\frac{1}{2}\mu_s g \Delta t_{\text{flight}}$ recovers true launch and impact speed without external radar, while restricting `PlatformBreakAudioController` to transient in-memory envelopes guarantees zero audio file recording. |
| **M5:** UK 8-Ball (`BLACKBALL`) & APA/BCAPL plain-language rule comparisons + playable tutorial layouts (`RulesContent.kt`, `TutorialsAndDrillsCatalog`, [ADR-0004](adr/0004-snooker-break-safety-planner-and-acoustic-break-speed.md)) | Players regularly switch between WPA/BCAPL call-pocket, APA, bar rules, and UK Blackball (free shot / two-visit penalties and baulk placement), so original plain-language summaries with synonym search stay free of copyrighted rule text while `"Try on Table Planner"` tutorial/drill layouts make every concept immediately interactive. |
| **Two-phase 2D/3D tutorial & walkthrough playback (`ShotTimelineAnimator`, `Interactive3DTutorialPlayer.kt`)** | Computing deterministic kinematic timelines (`0.0..1.0`) and 3D perspective projections in pure KMP `:engine` while keeping Compose Canvas rendering in `:composeApp` allows learners to inspect 4 annotated 2D stages first (Phase 1) and then orbit a 3D table and scrub the exact strike/impact/rebound frame slider with live spin & speed tweaks (Phase 2) completely offline and JVM-testable. |
| **Pure-KMP raw pixel & video table decoding (`RawImageTableDetector`, `ArgbImageFrame`)** | Operating on packed `IntArray` ARGB buffers in `:engine` makes HSV cloth segmentation (`TOURNAMENT_BLUE`, `SIMONIS_GREEN`, `CHAMPIONSHIP_RED`, `SLATE_GREY`), 4-corner quad extraction, connected-component ball blob classification, and multi-frame video tracking 100% JVM-testable offline and shareable across Android, iOS, and local `ml/datasets/` diagnostics with zero platform bitmap or AGPL dependencies. |
| **Cluster collision propagation & debounced landscape playback (`TableCollisionSimulator`, `FullscreenTableScreen`)** | Resolving post-impact multi-ball elastic scatter inside `:engine` updates each shot's `resultingLayout` so Step 2+ reflects broken clusters rather than static racks, while deferring multi-shot planning (`150 ms` debounce on `Dispatchers.Default`) during ball drags, exposing `[< Prev] / [Next >]` playback + pocket selection, and offering a landscape rail view keeps tabletop play fast and uncluttered. |

## Picking it up

- Install Android Studio (current stable) and use its bundled JDK (or JDK 17) to run `:engine:jvmTest`, `lint`, and `:composeApp:assembleDebug`.
- Read the [roadmap](roadmap.md), then the research note for the area you are working on.
- Keep the docs in step with the code: update the relevant research note or the roadmap in the same pull request, and update this file when the project's state changes.
- Check that every new dependency, model, dataset or asset has a licence compatible with MIT before adding it.
- Feature work goes on a branch and reaches `main` through a pull request.
